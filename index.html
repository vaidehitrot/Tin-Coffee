Pods, for mornings without a scale

The same Chikkamagaluru beans, ground for capsule brewing, then nitrogen-flushed and sealed so oxygen stays out. Ten capsules to a box.

Fits Caramelly machines and Nespresso® OriginalLine machines: Essenza Mini, Pixie, Inissia, CitiZ, Creatista, Lattissima and Espresso Pop. Not for Vertuo.

Whole bean, or ground for your brewer

Caramelly grinds to order for seven brew methods. Match the grind to the brewer and the rest of the recipe gets easy. These are starting points; taste and adjust one notch at a time.

Roasted on a Probat, one profile at a time

Every batch goes through a Probat drum roaster fitted with variable frequency drives on drum speed and exhaust. That control lets a light microlot and a dark espresso roast each hit their own curve, batch after batch.
